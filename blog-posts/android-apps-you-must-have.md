Having used the android Operating System for more than a decade and counting, I
believe my experience is vast enough to make this list of the must-have android
applications. Any application mentioned bellow is not for promotion purpose, but
purely based on my experience and unbiased preference over the years. Also note
that the list below is in no order. Let's go straight to the point.

# Top Android Applications You Should Install

- #### Ampere 

Have you ever plugged your phone for hours just to come back and wonder if it's
charging slower or something? You get confused whether it's the USB cable, the
charger (_charger-head_) or the charging port. Before you can know what may be
wrong with your phone, you have to plug and wait for like ten minutes to study
the charging speed every time you plug or change your charging equipment -
Ampere makes that easier. This simple yet essential app can read your device
charging and discharging speed. You can use it to test how fast a cable or
charger charger works without having to wait for ten minutes or thereabout.

- #### AutoResponder WA

Earlier before I discovered this app, I was using Tasker and Automate to achieve
what it gives out of the box. As the names says, this app can serve as a little
robot that monitors your WhatsApp notifications and respond to messages
automatically (as pre-configured). As an example/use-case, I receive averagely
25 "thank you" related messages daily which should attract equally 25 "you're
welcome" kinda response. I find it stressful typing this every time someone
thanks me so I set some processes using Tasker and Automate to trigger the
response, and that's what AutoResponder WA does too. I've tried AutoResponder WA
as well and it's quite straightforward and easy to operate but I think I'll
stick to my stressful Tasker processes. Lol. Just check out AutoResponder WA,
you may just love what it does.

- #### YouVersion | Yoruba English | Tecarta

If you're a Christian or you're interested in reading the Bible, these are three
different Bible applications I love for different reasons. They all offer
similar features except for the Yoruba English which is the only bilingual and
mono-versioned (KJV) Bible among them. The other two give you access to all
versions of Bible with audio and more. Some versions are paid on Tecarta Bible
while YouVersion gives you access to all versions for free if you agree to share
your name and email with the publishers of the versions. I've used Tecarta for
more than six years and the other two for less than three years.

- #### CamScanner

When it comes to scanning documents on the go and in the best possible quality,
CamScanner does the job perfectly. The scanner is rich in features and there are
some functionalities reserved for the premium version such as OCR (Optical
Character Recognition). Another beauty of it is that you can synchronize your
documents across devices.

- #### Clip Stack

Just like Ditto on Windows, Clip Stack provides everything needed in a clipboard
manager. This simple app monitors and keeps everything you copy on your phone
for easier reference and access. You can select some of your clipboard texts as
favorites, to appear on a separate tab. The texts copied two years+ ago can
still be accessed and searched in Clip Stack history if nothing happens to your
phone or if you have a backup. Clip Stack can be used as a Note/Todo app to jot
things like to-do list an all. To wrap it all, the app is really minimal and
doesn't take much memory.

- #### Codec

This is a technical word processor with a touch of styling and decoration. You
can convert texts to different formats including ASCII and even binary format.
Also, Codec can convert your texts to different fonts. You can check it out on
Google Play Store, it's a cool app and free to an extent.

- #### Copy Text On Screen

This does what its name says. Some website owners disable copy functionality on
their web pages, if you have Copy Text On Screen, you can just make a screenshot
and use the app to extract the texts out. Google Lens also does this which is
one of the stock apps that come with recent android versions. So if your phone
does not have Google Lens or does not support it, Copy Text On Screen is your
best bet.

- #### Droid Info

Droid Info shows you rich and detailed information about your android phone such as
your chip-set, fingerprint version, dpi, CPU architecture, your exact camera
quality, memory and storage sizes, available and supported sensors, and even thermal
information. With Droid Info, you will know more about your device. 

- #### FL Studio Mobile

I haven't really used this application, I just have it hanging around on my
phone. It's for music editing and making. A friend once said he used it to
extract beat out of a song and I think that's why I downloaded it. I just
haven't had time to explore yet. You can check it out, it's a paid app on Play Store.

- #### Edge Gesture

I started using this app two years ago and it's been making my life supper
easier. With Edge Gesture, you can perform several actions just by touching the
edges of your screen. It supports clicking, double-clicking, triple-clicking,
slide up, slide down, slide and hold, etc. You can even open an app or pull down
the notification panel by sliding your finger at the edge of your screen instead
of reaching to the top of the screen. Edge Gesture also has a customizable
floating button you can be explored and utilized. 

- #### KineMaster

I've used several other video editors on android but KineMaster stands out.
Especially if your chip-set is Qualcomm Snapdragon, you can have more
functionalities such as Green Background. It's a vast app awaiting editors who
can explore it. 

- #### Markor

Markor is an effective simple text editor. I love it because it can compile markdown and it does this the best way
it should. In fact, that's what I used to type this blog post. It offers the
best experience if you are ever cornered to write a markdown article on android.
It's handy and rich in features. 
- #### MP3 Video Converter

If you need to quickly take an audio out of a video, this simple piece is your
plug. It doesn't only extract audios but can trim and merge them. It does it job
and doesn't run in background stealthily. Good Play Store link.

- #### NetSpeed Indicator

What this app offers usually comes with phones these days but if your phone
doesn't have the feature to display your network speed (both uploading and
downloading speed) on your notification bar, I'm sure you want to use NetSpeed
Indicator to get that done. I've been using this app since the days of Techno
P5, it is very light and memory friendly. 

- #### Nickzom Calculator

If you're an engineering student or a science student who deals with Mathematics
and its surrounding subjects, the capabilities of this small but mighty
calculator will amaze you. The developer really did an amazing job. This
calculator can solve most engineering calculations and still show the steps. All
you need to provide are the parameters. And surprisingly, it's not even heavy.
You need to check it out for real. 

- #### Unified Remote Full and PC Remote

These apps can let you control your PC from your phone, exciting right? You can
be using your phone's screen as your laptop's track-pad. You can as well cast
your phone's screen to your PC and vise versa. You can use them as a remote camera -
after connecting your PC to your phone, what your PC camera is capturing can be
watched on your phone. Both apps are great at what they do and they have diverse
functionalities you can't finish exploring in hours.

- #### QuickShortcutMaker and Power Shortcuts

There are some functionalities on android phones that you'll need to tap screen
up to five or six times before you get to where they are located in settings.
For example, if you want to adjust your phone's font-size or you want to add
another finger to your registered fingerprints, you have to go through Settings
and click the next button and another one before you get there, but with either
of these apps, you can simply assign an icon to that operation and maybe pull it
to your home-screen or app drawers with a customizable name and icon just like
an app-icon. Both are free on Play Store.

- #### PVR Remote

I still wonder why this app was named PVR Remote instead of maybe DSTV Remote which is more straightforward. The app is simple a DSTV decoder remote control. If your phone has Infrared or can use the 
IR-dependent remote controls, then here is an app for you to control your DSTV
decoders. It works perfectly just like the hard remote control if not better. It's
available on Google Play Store, free.

- #### Quotes Widget

This is a tiny app that does just one thing - displays quotes on your home
screen. It is pre-loaded with 2000 quotes from different categories and wise scholars. The widget that can be
neatly seen on your phone screen every time you unlock your phone. You can
also add more quotes locally and manually, and get them displayed. I love it because it simply
does its job without ads or distractions.

- #### Screenshot Easy Pro

Screen-shooting comes with every android phone of course but not all android
phones can make a long screenshot by default; hence, the reason I have this app. It's neat and has more functionalities you may love.
It's a paid app but there is a free version as well. Check it.

- #### SD Maid

If you feel your phone storage is filled up and nothing else to safely delete,
try using SD Maid, you'll realize how much deletable junks you have hidden in your
storage. SD Maid is customizable enough to not touch anything you don't want it
to touch. It can clean your most stubborn app caches and can be programmed to periodically
perform this storage cleaning automatically, just like a maid that it is. With
SD Maid, rarely will you go out of storage and your important files are always
safe. It's free to an extent on Play Store.

- #### Sleep Sounds

This app is a collection of different categories of serene, quality, and suiting sounds for
different moods. Apart for sleeping, I use this app as noise cancellation maybe
if I want to read in and concentrate in a noisy environment. It has the perfect
sound for every mood, and highly customizable. Try it out, you may fall in love
with it.

- #### Unit Converter

This is a box of tools. You can convert almost all measurements and units
ranging from length to weight to size and volume. You can as well convert
currencies easily and the currency conversion rates get updated frequently.

- #### Mi Remote and Zaza Remote

These remote controls work only work if your phone has infrared. You can set up a
remote control for any kind of appliance and electronics such as TV, AC, fan,
etc. Choosing either of the two is purely preference. I have both because one came with my Xiaomi phone.

- #### iQuran Pro

Among all the Quran applications I have ever come across, iQuran Pro renders the
best functionalities. The major reason I fell in love with this invaluable app
is for its transliteration. You don't compulsorily have to be able to read
Arabic before you can feel the glimpse of pronouncing Arabic words. It has
translations as well, in different international languages and by well respected
reciters. If you ever have a need to use a soft Quran, iQuran Pro will
definitely serve you well. It is a paid app on Play Store but it has a
free lite version.

- ### Cube ACR

This is a useful alternative if your phone does not have an inbuilt call
recorder. I've tried many other call recorders but Cube ACR seems to offer more
and works efficiently without getting killed by battery managers. Get your conversations automatically recorded by Cube ACR and have zero worries if you need to listen to what someone told you via phone call.

- ### Fake Call

In a few words, Fake Call fakes calls. Some situations may arise where you
desperately pray a call comes through on your phone so you can excuse yourself
from a tiring meeting or escape a boring gathering - Fake Call becomes a savior. I've
used this app to escape some never-ending choir rehearsals in the past.
The app can go as far as talking back to you if you pick the fake call - just like a
real call. Fake voice can be preset which makes it more "real".

- ### Inputting+

I still wonder why this app is so unpopular. Well, maybe because its importance
is not really obvious even when it can be a life-saving tool when it's needed.
Have you ever typed a super-long message in maybe an online forum textbox or on
WhatsApp reply-box but one way or the other, it got cleared and you just had to
retype all that? It's happened to me on several occasions and that's why I dug
this app out. Inputting+ can be used to undo lost texts. It monitors your
keystrokes as you type every alphabet on all apps. Of course, you can configure
it to your taste by black-listing or white-listing some apps as you wish.

- ### NOVA Launcher Prime

This is the best android launcher I've ever used. It effortlessly gives you
full control over your phone with rich customizations and attractive
look-and-feel. If you are a good app explorer, you can use this app to make a
god out of your device. It is a paid app on Google Play Store but has a free version.

While there are other important apps, to mention few, and to focus on apps everyone can easily utilize, I
intentionally excluded some apps that cannot be fully enjoyed without having
root access, e.g Titanium Backup, Mobile Uncle, Lucky Patcher, Tasker, Macro Droid,
Automate, etc. Most of them are not on Google Play Store though.
